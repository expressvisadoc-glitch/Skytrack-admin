==================================================
1. BACKEND LEAVE RULES
==================================================

The backend is now the source of truth for all leave calculations.

Rules:

1. Monday-Saturday are working days.
2. Sunday is excluded from leave-day calculation.
3. Casual Leave accrues automatically.
4. Sick Leave accrues automatically according to the backend accrual system.
5. Employees can request leave even if their paid balance is insufficient.
6. Approval consumes available paid leave first.
7. Any remaining approved leave becomes LOP.
8. LOP does not reduce the paid leave balance.
9. LOP is NOT a separate leave type.
10. Leave type remains:
    - casual
    - sick

Example:

Employee balance:
Casual = 2 days

Leave request:
Monday-Friday = 5 working days

After approval:

leaveDays = 5
paidLeaveDays = 2
lopDays = 3

Remaining balance = 0.

DO NOT reproduce this calculation in the frontend.

The backend/database performs this calculation.
