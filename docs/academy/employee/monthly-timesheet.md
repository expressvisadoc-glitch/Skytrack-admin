# Downloading your timesheet

## What this feature does

Produces a PDF of your attendance for a month, which you can save, print or share.

## Who can use it

Employees, for their own record.

## Prerequisites

Choose the month you want first.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Monthly Overview**.
2. Choose the month.
3. Select the timesheet download option.
4. On the phone the PDF opens in a viewer, from which you can share it.

## Expected outcome

A PDF listing each day with its punches and hours.

## Common mistakes

- **Confusing a timesheet with a payslip.** A timesheet shows *time*. A payslip shows *money* and only
  exists once the month has been closed. See [Downloading your payslip](employee.payslip.download).
- **Downloading mid-month and treating it as final.** The current month is still changing.

## Troubleshooting

If the PDF does not open on the phone, make sure you have a PDF viewer, or download from the web portal.
