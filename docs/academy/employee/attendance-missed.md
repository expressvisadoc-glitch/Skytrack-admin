# What happens if you forget to check out

## What this feature does

If you check in but never check out, SkyTrack closes the day for you rather than leaving it open forever.

## Who can use it

Employees — this happens automatically.

## What actually happens

- The day is closed automatically once it is well past.
- **You are not docked pay for it.** A missed check-out carries no deduction.
- Your recorded hours for that day will not reflect when you actually left.

## Expected outcome

Your attendance history has no permanently open days.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming you have lost a day's pay.** You have not. A forgotten check-out is not an absence; you checked
  in, so the day counts as present.
- **Trying to check out the next morning.** You cannot check out for a previous day. Ask an administrator.

## Troubleshooting

If the hours matter — for example you worked late — ask an administrator to correct the check-out time on
the **Attendance** screen. See [Entering or correcting attendance](administrator.attendance.manual).
