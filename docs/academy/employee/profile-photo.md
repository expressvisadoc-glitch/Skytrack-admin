# Changing your profile photo

## What this feature does

Sets the photo shown next to your name.

## Who can use it

Employees, for their own profile.

## Prerequisites

On the phone, permission to use the camera or photo library when asked.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **My Account**.
2. Select your photo or the placeholder.
3. Take a photo or choose one from your library.
4. Confirm.

## Expected outcome

The new photo appears on your profile and wherever your name is shown.

## Common mistakes

- **Denying camera access and then wondering why nothing happens.** Allow it in your phone's settings for
  SkyTrack.
- **Expecting it to change your details.** A photo is only a photo.

## Troubleshooting

If the upload fails, check your connection and try a smaller image.
