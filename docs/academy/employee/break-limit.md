# How long a break can be

## What this feature does

Your organisation sets an **allowed break** length. The app shows it beside your break total — for example
*Break (1h Max)*.

## Who can use it

Employees. Administrators set the value in **Payroll Settings**.

## What happens if you go over

**Nothing is deducted from your pay.** The allowed break is a **reminder only**. If your break runs longer,
you may get a notification, but SkyTrack does not reduce your salary for it.

Your longer break does reduce your recorded **working hours**, because hours are
`(check-out − check-in) − break time`.

## Expected outcome

You know the expectation, and you know that exceeding it is a matter for your manager rather than an
automatic deduction.

## Common mistakes

- **Thinking a long break costs you money directly.** It does not. Only absence, unpaid leave and lateness
  reduce pay.
- **Thinking the limit applies per break.** It is about your break time overall for the day.

## Troubleshooting

If the limit shown looks wrong, ask an administrator to check **Payroll Settings**.
