# Reading your monthly attendance

## What this feature does

Shows every day of a month with what was recorded: your check-in and check-out times, break time and total
hours.

## Who can use it

Employees, for their own record only.

## Prerequisites

None.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Monthly Overview** (on the phone, the calendar icon).
2. Use the month control to move between months.
3. Select a day to see its punches.

## What the days mean

| Day shows | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Times and hours | A normal worked day |
| Marked late | You checked in after your shift start |
| Nothing recorded | No check-in. Either a non-working day, approved leave, or an absence |
| Holiday or week-off | A non-working day — nothing was expected |
| Still working | You have checked in and not yet checked out |

## Expected outcome

You can answer "what did I do on the 12th?" without asking anyone.

## Common mistakes

- **Reading a blank working day as a system fault.** A blank day with no leave is recorded as an absence and
  will reduce your pay. Raise it the same week, not at month end.
- **Expecting today's totals to be final.** Today is only complete once you have checked out.

## Troubleshooting

If a day looks wrong, ask an administrator — they can correct a check-in or check-out time.
