# Your profile and reporting manager

## What this feature does

Shows your employee record: name, employee ID, email, department, designation, work shift, joining date and
reporting manager.

## Who can use it

Employees, for their own record.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **My Account** (on the phone, the person icon).
2. Read your details.

## What you can change yourself

| You can change | You cannot change |
| :--- | :--- |
| Your profile photo | Name, employee ID, email |
| Your password | Department, designation, work shift |
| Phone notification setting | Reporting manager, joining date, role |

Anything in the right-hand column is changed by an administrator.

## Expected outcome

You know who your reporting manager is and which shift you are on.

## Why the shift matters

Your **work shift** sets your expected start time, which decides whether a check-in counts as late. If the
shift shown is wrong, raise it — it directly affects your pay once late rules apply.

## Common mistakes

- **Trying to edit the department or shift.** Only an administrator can.
- **Ignoring a blank joining date.** If it is missing, the whole month is counted for pay. Ask an
  administrator to set it.

## Troubleshooting

If your reporting manager shows as unassigned, ask an administrator to assign one.
