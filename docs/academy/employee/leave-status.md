# Tracking your leave request

## What this feature does

Lists your leave requests and their current state.

## Who can use it

Employees, for their own requests.

## The four states

| State | Meaning | You can |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pending** | Waiting for a decision | Cancel it |
| **Approved** | Granted; your balance is updated | Nothing — ask an administrator |
| **Rejected** | Declined, usually with a comment | Apply again |
| **Cancelled** | You withdrew it | Apply again |

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Leave Requests**.
2. Look at the history below your balances.
3. Select a request to see its dates, type, days and any manager comment.

## Expected outcome

For an approved request you can see how it was split — how many days were **paid** and how many became
**loss of pay**.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming approved means fully paid.** Check the paid and LOP split; days beyond your balance are unpaid.
- **Waiting for a reply instead of reading the comment.** A rejection usually carries the reason.

## Troubleshooting

If a request has been pending a long time, contact your administrator — SkyTrack does not approve anything
automatically.
