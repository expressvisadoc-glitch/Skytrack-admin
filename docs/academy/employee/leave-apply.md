# How to apply for leave

## What this feature does

Sends a leave request to your administrator for a decision.

## Who can use it

Employees, for themselves.

## Prerequisites

Know your dates. You do not need a balance — leave beyond your balance is simply unpaid.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Leave Requests**.
2. Start a new request.
3. Choose the **type**. A type with no balance left is greyed out — see Common mistakes.
4. Choose the **from** and **to** dates. For one day, use the same date twice.
5. Tick **half day** if you need only half. See [Applying for a half day](employee.leave.halfday).
6. Give a short reason.
7. Submit.

## Expected outcome

The request appears in your history as **Pending**. You will be told when it is decided.

## How it will be split

When it is approved, days covered by your balance are **paid** and the rest become **loss of pay**. Two days
against a one-day balance gives one paid day and one unpaid day — and one day's pay is deducted.

Days falling on a week-off or a public holiday are not counted at all.

## Common mistakes

- **A greyed-out leave type.** It means that balance is zero. To take the time anyway, apply as **Loss of
  Pay**. See [A leave type is greyed out](troubleshooting.leave.zero-balance).
- **Applying for dates that overlap an existing request.** SkyTrack refuses overlapping requests. Cancel or
  amend the first one.
- **Expecting instant approval.** A request stays pending until an administrator decides.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| "These dates overlap an existing leave request." | You already have a request covering those dates. |
| The type is greyed out | No balance left for that type. |
