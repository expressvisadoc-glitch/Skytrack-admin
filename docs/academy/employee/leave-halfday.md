# Applying for a half day

## What this feature does

Books half a day of leave. It counts as **0.5 days** against your balance and, if unpaid, as half a day's
deduction.

## Who can use it

Employees.

## Prerequisites

A single date.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Leave Requests** and start a new request.
2. Set the same date for **from** and **to**.
3. Tick the **half day** option.
4. Choose the type, give a reason, and submit.

## Expected outcome

The request shows **0.5 days**. Approved and paid, it uses half a day of balance; approved and unpaid, it
deducts half a day's pay.

## Common mistakes

- **Using a half day for a short day.** Leaving early is not deducted at all, so you may not need leave.
  SkyTrack has no short-hours rule.
- **Expecting to choose morning or afternoon.** SkyTrack records half a day, not which half.
- **Ticking half day across a date range.** A half day applies to a single date.

## Troubleshooting

If the request shows 1 day instead of 0.5, the half-day option was not applied — cancel while it is still
pending and re-submit.
