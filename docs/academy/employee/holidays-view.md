# Seeing upcoming holidays

## What this feature does

Lists the holidays your organisation observes — both statutory public holidays and any your company has
declared. Both count the same way.

## Who can use it

Everyone.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Public Holidays**.
2. Scroll the list of upcoming dates.

## Expected outcome

You can see which days you are not expected to work.

## Why it matters

A holiday is **not a working day**, so:

- You are not expected to check in, and no absence is recorded.
- It does not use leave. Leave spanning a holiday does not count that day.
- It reduces the month's working days, which slightly **increases** the value of each working day.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying for leave across a holiday and expecting it to be used.** It is not counted, which is in your
  favour.
- **Checking in on a holiday and expecting a late mark.** Non-working days never produce a late mark.

## Troubleshooting

If a holiday your organisation observes is missing, tell an administrator — only they can add it.
