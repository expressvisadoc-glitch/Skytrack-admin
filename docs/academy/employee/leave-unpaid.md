# What loss of pay (LOP) means for you

## What this feature does

**Loss of pay** is leave you take that is not covered by a balance. The time off is granted; the day is not
paid.

## Who can use it

Employees.

## When leave becomes LOP

- You apply for more days than your balance covers — the excess is LOP.
- You apply directly as **Loss of Pay**.

The split is decided **when the request is approved**, against your balance at that moment.

## What it costs

One LOP day costs **one day's pay**, where a day's pay is your monthly salary divided by that month's
working days.

For example, on a monthly salary of ₹20,000 in a month with 24 working days, one LOP day is about **₹833**.

## Expected outcome

Your approved request shows its paid and LOP days, and the LOP days appear as a deduction on your salary.

## Common mistakes

- **Thinking a rejected request costs money.** It does not. Only **approved** leave can be LOP.
- **Thinking LOP days on a week-off or holiday count.** They do not. Only working days count.
- **Expecting the deduction to appear immediately on an old month.** A month that has been closed keeps its
  figures.

## Troubleshooting

If a deduction looks wrong, check the paid/LOP split on the approved request first. An administrator can
adjust a balance, but a closed month must be reopened to change.
