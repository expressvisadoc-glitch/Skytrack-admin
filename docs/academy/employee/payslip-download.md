# Downloading your payslip

> **Not available yet.** This feature is built and tested but is not in the version you are using today.

## What this feature does

Gives you a PDF payslip for a month that has been closed: your details, working and paid days, gross,
itemised deductions and net pay, on the company letterhead.

## Who can use it

Employees, for their own payslips only.

## Prerequisites

**The month must have been closed by an administrator.** Payslips exist only for closed months — there is no
payslip for the month in progress.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Monthly Overview**.
2. Find the payslips section.
3. Choose the month and download it. On the phone it opens in a viewer and can be shared.

## Expected outcome

A PDF showing gross pay, each deduction in days and rupees, total deductions and net pay.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for the current month.** It is not closed yet. Use the salary card instead.
- **Expecting it to change later.** A payslip is frozen. If a correction is needed, an administrator reopens
  the month and a **new version** is issued — you then see the latest version.
- **Confusing it with a timesheet.** A timesheet shows time; a payslip shows money.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| No payslips listed | No month has been closed yet. |
| A month is missing | That month has not been closed. |
| The figures differ from what you remember | The month was reopened and corrected; you are seeing the newer version. |
