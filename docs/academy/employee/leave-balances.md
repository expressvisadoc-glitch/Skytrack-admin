# Understanding your leave balances

## What this feature does

Shows how much paid leave you have left, by type.

## Who can use it

Employees, for their own balances.

## The three types

| Type | Has a balance | Paid |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Casual** | Yes | Yes, while you have balance |
| **Sick** | Yes | Yes, while you have balance |
| **Loss of Pay (LOP)** | **No** | **No** — unpaid |

LOP has no balance because it is not an allowance. It is what any leave becomes once your balance runs out.

## How a balance is worked out

```
balance = allocated + adjustments − used
```

## Expected outcome

You can see, before applying, how many paid days you have.

## Common mistakes

- **Thinking a zero balance blocks leave.** It does not. You can still take the time — it is simply unpaid.
- **Expecting LOP to show a number.** It never will.
- **Assuming a balance covers a whole request.** A two-day request against a one-day balance gives you one
  paid day and one unpaid day.

## Troubleshooting

If a balance looks wrong, ask an administrator — they can adjust it and the change is recorded.
