# Turning phone alerts on or off

> **Not available yet.** This feature is built and tested but is not in the version you are using today.

## What this feature does

One switch controlling **all** SkyTrack notifications on your phone. It does not affect the in-app inbox.

## Who can use it

Employees, on the phone app.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **My Account** in the app.
2. Find **Punch-in Alerts**.
3. Switch it off to stop phone notifications, or on to resume them.

It is **on** by default.

## Expected outcome

With it off, no SkyTrack notifications appear on your phone. The bell inbox still fills up normally.

## Two separate switches

| Switch | Where | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Punch-in Alerts** | SkyTrack → My Account | SkyTrack's own setting |
| **App notification permission** | Your phone's settings | Android's permission |

**Both must be on** for a notification to reach you. If you denied the permission when you installed the
app, turning the SkyTrack switch on is not enough.

## Common mistakes

- **Turning the switch on and still seeing nothing** because the Android permission is denied. Check your
  phone's app settings for SkyTrack.
- **Expecting it to stop emails.** It only controls phone notifications.
