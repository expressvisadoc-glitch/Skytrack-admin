# Your first day: check in, break, check out

## What this feature does

Shows one normal day end to end, so you know the rhythm.

## Who can use it

Employees.

## Prerequisites

You can sign in, and you are at the office.

## Step-by-step instructions

**When you arrive**

1. Open SkyTrack. The big circle says **Check-In** and the status reads **Ready**.
2. Select the circle. Your position is checked, and your check-in time is recorded.
3. The circle changes to show you are working, with a running timer.

**When you take a break**

4. Select **Break**. The timer pauses and the app shows you are on a break.
5. When you come back, select the same button to **resume work**.

**When you leave**

6. Select **Check-Out**. Your position is checked again.
7. The day closes and shows your total hours and break time.

## Expected outcome

At the end of the day you see your check-in time, your break total and your total hours — for example
`Check-In 09:50 AM · Break 45:01 · Total Hours 7h 15m`.

## Common mistakes

- **Forgetting to resume after a break.** You cannot check out while a break is running.
- **Forgetting to check out.** The day is closed automatically. You are not penalised, but your hours for
  that day will not be exact.
- **Checking in from the car park or the gate.** You may be outside the permitted radius. Go inside first.
