# Finding your way around SkyTrack

## What this feature does

Everything in SkyTrack is reached from the menu on the left. What is in that menu depends on your role.

## Who can use it

Everyone.

## The employee menu — five items

| Item | What is there |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Attendance & Breaks** | The punch clock. Check in, take a break, check out. Your starting screen. |
| **Leave Requests** | Your balances, applying for leave, and the status of past requests. |
| **Monthly Overview** | Your calendar, daily punches, hours, timesheet and payslips. |
| **Public Holidays** | Upcoming company and public holidays. |
| **My Account** | Your profile, photo, reporting manager and password. |

On the phone the same five live in the bar along the bottom.

## The administrator menu — ten items

| Item | What is there |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Dashboard** | Who is in, out or on break right now. Your starting screen. |
| **Employees** | The staff directory; add and edit people. |
| **Attendance** | Everybody's attendance; correct a record. |
| **Leave Management** | Requests waiting for a decision. |
| **Public Holidays** | The holiday calendar. |
| **Settings** | Your own administrator profile and password. |
| **Organization** | Departments, work shifts and reporting managers. |
| **Office Geofence** | Where staff are allowed to check in from. |
| **Payroll** | The monthly salary run; lock a month; payslips. |
| **Payroll Settings** | Week-offs, lateness rules and break length. |

Super administrators also see **Admin Management**.

## Expected outcome

You can find any screen in one step from the menu.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for Payroll as an employee.** Your pay is on **Attendance & Breaks** (the salary card) and
  **Monthly Overview** (payslips). The Payroll screen is an administrator tool.
- **Looking for employee details under Attendance.** Open **Employees** and select the person; the
  attendance screen is organised by day, not by person.
