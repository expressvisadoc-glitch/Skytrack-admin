# Understanding roles and what each one can do

## What this feature does

Your **role** decides which version of SkyTrack you see and what you are allowed to do. It is set on your
employee record by an administrator; you cannot change your own role.

## Who can use it

Everyone — knowing your role explains what you can see.

## Prerequisites

None.

## The three roles

| Role | Where you work | What you can do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Employee** | Phone app and web portal | Your own attendance, breaks, leave, holidays, profile and pay |
| **Administrator** | Web portal | Everything an employee can do for themselves, plus: everyone's attendance, leave decisions, employees, departments, shifts, holidays, office location and payroll |
| **Super administrator** | Web portal | Everything an administrator can do, plus managing administrator accounts and resetting other people's passwords |

## Expected outcome

You know why your menu looks the way it does.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming a missing menu item is a bug.** Only super administrators see **Admin Management**. If you are
  an administrator and cannot see it, that is correct.
- **Assuming an administrator can see your password.** Nobody can. A super administrator can *reset* it, not
  read it.

## Troubleshooting

If you see a screen that will not load, or a message saying only administrators can do something, see
[“Only administrators can…”](troubleshooting.permission.denied).
