# Installing the SkyTrack app on your phone

## What this feature does

Puts SkyTrack on your Android phone so you can check in and out from where you work.

## Who can use it

Employees with an Android phone running **Android 8.0 or newer**.

## Prerequisites

- An Android phone (there is no iPhone app — iPhone users use the web portal).
- The installation file or link from your administrator.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open the link or file your administrator sent you.
2. If Android asks, allow installation from that source.
3. Open **SkyTrack** once it is installed.
4. Sign in with your employee ID and password.
5. When asked, allow **Location**. Choose **Precise**, not approximate — an approximate position is usually
   not accurate enough to check in.
6. When asked, allow **Notifications** so you get shift reminders.

## Expected outcome

The app opens on your attendance screen showing your name and today's date.

## Common mistakes

- **Choosing "Approximate" location.** It is rarely precise enough and your check-in will be refused.
- **Denying notifications and then wondering why no reminders arrive.** You can turn them back on in
  **My Account**.
- **Installing on an iPhone.** There is no iPhone app. Use the web portal.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| Android warns the app is from an unknown source | Expected for an app installed outside the Play Store. Continue if your administrator sent it. |
| The app will not install | Check you have free storage. |
| Location is denied and check-in fails | See [SkyTrack cannot see your location](troubleshooting.checkin.location-permission). |
