# How to sign in with your employee ID

## What this feature does

Signs you in to SkyTrack. You can use either your **employee ID** or your **email address**, with the same
password, on both the phone app and the web portal.

## Who can use it

Everyone with an active SkyTrack account.

## Prerequisites

- Your employee ID, for example `SKY007`, or the email address on your record.
- Your password.
- An internet connection.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open the SkyTrack app, or go to the SkyTrack web address in a browser.
2. In **EMPLOYEE ID**, type your employee ID or your email address.
3. In **PASSWORD**, type your password. Use the eye icon to check what you typed.
4. Tick **Remember me** if this is your own device, so you do not have to sign in every time.
5. Select **Sign In**.

## Expected outcome

- **Employees** land on the attendance screen, with your name and a punch clock.
- **Administrators** land on the Dashboard.

Where you land is decided by your role; you do not choose it.

## Common mistakes

- **Typing the employee ID with the wrong case or spacing.** `SKY007` — no spaces.
- **Using "Remember me" on a shared computer.** It keeps you signed in. Do not use it on a shared machine.
- **Expecting an admin menu as an employee.** Employees see five menu items; administrators see ten or more.
  That is correct, not a fault.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Invalid credentials" | Check the ID and password. Passwords are case-sensitive. |
| You forgot your password | Select **Forgot Password?**, or ask an administrator to reset it. |
| You are signed out unexpectedly | See [You were signed out unexpectedly](troubleshooting.session.expired). |
