# Changing your password

## What this feature does

Changes your own password. You must know your current one.

## Who can use it

Everyone, for their own account. Only a **super administrator** can reset somebody else's password.

## Prerequisites

Your current password.

## Step-by-step instructions

**On the phone**

1. Open **My Account**.
2. Select **Change password**.
3. Enter your current password, then your new one twice.
4. Confirm.

**In the web portal**

1. Employees: open **My Account**. Administrators: open **Settings**.
2. Find the password section and enter your current and new passwords.
3. Save.

## Password rules

At least **8 characters**, with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a symbol.

## Expected outcome

Your password changes and **you are signed out everywhere**. Sign in again with the new password on each
device.

## Common mistakes

- **Not knowing your current password.** Then you cannot change it yourself — ask a super administrator to
  reset it.
- **Being surprised at being signed out on your phone.** That is deliberate: changing a password ends every
  session on the account.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Current password is incorrect" | Check caps lock. |
| The new password is rejected | It does not meet the rules above. |
| You are locked out | Ask a super administrator for a reset. |
