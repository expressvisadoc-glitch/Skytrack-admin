# How SkyTrack keeps you informed

> **Not available yet.** This feature is built and tested but is not in the version you are using today.
> This article is here so it is ready when the feature is released.

## What this feature does

Sends you reminders and tells you when something that affects you has changed, in two places:

- **On your phone**, as a notification.
- **In the app**, in the bell inbox — whether or not phone notifications are on.

## Who can use it

Employees get reminders about their own day. Administrators get alerts in the web portal's bell panel.

## What you will be told about

| You get | When |
| :--- | :--- |
| Shift reminder | Shortly before your shift starts |
| Not checked in | Your shift has started and you have not checked in |
| Marked absent | A working day passed with no check-in and no leave |
| Late check-in | You checked in after your shift start |
| Break running long | Your break has passed the allowed length |
| Missed checkout | You did not check out |
| Leave approved or rejected | Your manager decided |
| Holiday announced | A holiday is added to the calendar |
| Payslip ready | A month has been closed |

## Expected outcome

You hear about what matters without having to open the app and check.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting a confirmation every time you check in.** SkyTrack does not do that by design. It tells you
  when something needs attention, not when things go normally.
