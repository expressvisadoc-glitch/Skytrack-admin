# Switching between light and dark

## What this feature does

Switches the web portal between a light and a dark appearance. It changes nothing about your data.

## Who can use it

Everyone, in the web portal.

## Prerequisites

None.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Look at the top bar, to the right.
2. Select the sun or moon icon.

The icon shows what you will switch **to**: a moon means selecting it turns dark on.

## Expected outcome

The portal changes appearance immediately and stays that way next time.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for it in the phone app.** The app follows your phone's own setting.
