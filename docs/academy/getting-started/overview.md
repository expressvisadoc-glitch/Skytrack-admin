# What SkyTrack is and what it does for you

## What this feature does

SkyTrack records when you work, when you take leave, and what that means for your pay. It replaces a paper
register or a spreadsheet with one system that everybody sees the same way.

Three things happen in SkyTrack:

- **Attendance** — you check in when you arrive, take breaks, and check out when you leave.
- **Leave** — you apply for leave, your manager decides, and your balance updates.
- **Pay** — your month's salary is worked out from the attendance that actually happened.

## Who can use it

Everyone in the organisation, with different views:

| You are | You use | You see |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| An employee | The phone app or the web portal | Your own attendance, leave, holidays and pay |
| An administrator | The web portal | Everybody's attendance and leave, plus settings |
| A super administrator | The web portal | The above, plus administrator accounts |

You only ever see your own records unless you are an administrator.

## Prerequisites

An employee ID (for example `SKY007`) and a password, given to you when your account was created.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Sign in — see [How to sign in with your employee ID](getting-started.signin).
2. Look at the left-hand menu. That is everything you can do.
3. Start with **Attendance & Breaks**, where you check in and out.

## Expected outcome

You can sign in and find the screen you need.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting SkyTrack to track you during the day.** It does not. Your location is read only at the moment
  you check in or out, and at no other time.
- **Looking for other people's records.** As an employee you can only see your own.
