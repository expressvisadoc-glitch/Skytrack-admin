# Exporting payroll for the accountant

> **Not available yet.** This feature is built and tested but is not in the version you are using today.

## What this feature does

Downloads every employee's figures for a month as a CSV file.

## Who can use it

Administrators and super administrators.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Payroll** and select the month.
2. Choose **Export CSV**.
3. Open it in a spreadsheet.

## What the file contains

Employee ID and name, designation, month, payslip number and version, monthly gross, working days, paid
days, absence days and amount, unpaid leave days and amount, the late-penalty option in use, late days,
late minutes charged, late days equivalent and amount, total deductions, net pay, and a status.

## Expected outcome

A file your accountant can work from directly.

## Export after locking

Export a **locked** month wherever possible. An open month is still provisional and the figures will move.
A locked month's export matches the payslips exactly.

## Remember what is not in it

SkyTrack calculates attendance-based pay only. **No statutory deductions** — PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS
— are included. Net pay here means gross minus attendance deductions, not take-home after tax.

Tell your accountant this once; it prevents an expensive misunderstanding.

## Common mistakes

- **Sending an open month's export as final.**
- **Treating "net pay" as take-home.** It is not.
- **Emailing it without thought.** It contains everybody's salary.
