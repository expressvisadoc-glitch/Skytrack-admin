# What can reduce your pay

> **Not available yet.** This feature is built and tested but is not in the version you are using today.

## What this feature does

Lists exactly what SkyTrack deducts for. The list is short and complete.

## The three deductions

| Deduction | When | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unapproved absence** | A working day with no check-in and no approved leave | One day's pay |
| **Unpaid (LOP) leave** | The part of approved leave beyond your balance | One day's pay per day |
| **Late arrival** | Checking in after your shift start | Depends on the rule in use |

```
net pay = earned gross − absences − unpaid leave − late deductions
```

Never below zero, rounded to the rupee.

## What is deliberately **not** deducted

| Not deducted | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Leaving early or short hours | There is no short-hours rule |
| Forgetting to check out | The day is closed automatically; no penalty |
| A long break | The allowed break is a reminder only |
| Working on a holiday or week-off | Nothing changes |
| Statutory deductions — PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS | SkyTrack does not calculate these at all |

SkyTrack produces gross, attendance deductions and net. Any statutory deduction your organisation makes
happens elsewhere.

## Expected outcome

If your pay is lower than expected, it is one of three things, and your salary card names the dates.

## Common mistakes

- **Blaming short hours.** They cost nothing.
- **Assuming a rejected leave request caused it.** Only approved leave can be unpaid. A day with no
  attendance and no approved leave is an absence, which is the real cause.
