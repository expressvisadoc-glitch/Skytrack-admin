# You are already checked in

## Symptom

You press check in and are told you are already checked in, or nothing appears to happen.

## Cause

You have a check-in for today. SkyTrack records **one** check-in per day; a second press changes nothing.

This often happens when a first press looked like it failed — slow network — but actually succeeded.

## Fix

Nothing to fix. Check the screen: it should show your check-in time and a running timer.

## Expected outcome

Your original check-in time stands. Pressing again never overwrites it, so you cannot accidentally make
yourself look later than you were.

## If the time recorded is wrong

You cannot change it yourself. Ask an administrator to correct it on the **Attendance** screen.

## Common mistakes

- **Pressing several times on a slow connection** and worrying you created duplicates. You cannot — SkyTrack
  keeps one record per day.
- **Expecting to check in again after checking out.** The day is closed.
