# You were signed out unexpectedly

## Symptom

You are returned to the sign-in screen without having signed out.

## Causes

| Cause | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Your password was changed** | On any device, by you or by a super administrator. This ends **every** session on the account. |
| **The session expired** | Normal after a long period. |
| **Your account was deactivated** | You will not be able to sign in again. |
| **You did not tick "Remember me"** | Sessions are shorter. |

## Fix

Sign in again. If your password was changed, use the new one.

## Expected outcome

You sign back in and carry on. **Nothing is lost** — your attendance and leave are on the server, not in the
app.

## Does it affect an open day?

No. If you were checked in, you still are. Sign back in and the app shows your real state, because the
server holds it.

## Common mistakes

- **Panicking that a check-in was lost.** It was not.
- **Checking in again after signing back in.** Look first: you are probably already checked in.
