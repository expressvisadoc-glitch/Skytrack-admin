# You cannot check out during a break

## Symptom

Check-out is refused with *"Please resume working before checking out."*

## Cause

A break is still running. SkyTrack will not close a day with an open break, because the break would have no
end and your hours could not be worked out.

## Fix

1. Select the break button to **resume work**.
2. Then select **Check-Out**.

## Expected outcome

The break is closed, its length is added to your day's break total, and the check-out goes through.

## Why it is enforced

A break with no end would either run forever or have to be guessed. Requiring you to resume keeps your hours
exact.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting a break instead of checking out at the end of the day.** Check out directly.
- **Closing the app mid-break and assuming it ended.** It does not — the break keeps running until you
  resume.

## If you forgot overnight

Tell an administrator. They can correct the day's record.
