# Spotting absences and late arrivals

## What this feature does

Helps you find the exceptions — absences and late check-ins — early, rather than discovering them at month
end when they have become a payroll dispute.

## Who can use it

Administrators and super administrators.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Attendance** and move through recent dates.
2. Look for working days with **no check-in** and **no approved leave** — those are absences.
3. Look for days marked **late**.
4. Correct anything genuinely wrong now, while the month is still open.

## Expected outcome

No surprises at month end.

## Why timing matters

| When you find it | What it takes to fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| Same week | Edit the attendance. Done. |
| Month still open | Edit the attendance; figures recalculate. |
| **After the month is locked** | Reopen the month with a reason, correct it, lock again — producing a new payslip version |

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for employees to report problems.** Many never check their own record until payday.
- **Assuming a blank day is leave.** Leave only counts if it was **approved**. A verbal arrangement that was
  never entered is an absence.
- **Correcting a late mark by editing the shift.** It will not work — shift changes apply only to future
  days. Correct the check-in time instead.
