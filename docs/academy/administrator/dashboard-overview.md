# Reading the dashboard

## What this feature does

The dashboard is the administrator's starting screen: today's workforce at a glance, updating as people
punch.

## Who can use it

Administrators and super administrators.

## What it shows

| Panel | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Workforce counts | How many are checked in, on a break, or have not arrived |
| On break | Who is on a break right now, with a pulsing indicator |
| Today's roster | Each employee and their current state |

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Sign in as an administrator; the dashboard opens automatically.
2. Select an employee to open their details.

## Expected outcome

Within a few seconds of arriving you know who is present.

## Common mistakes

- **Reading "not checked in" as absent.** Early in the day it usually just means they have not arrived. An
  absence is only decided once the working day is over.
- **Using it as a historical record.** It is today. For other days use **Attendance**.

## Troubleshooting

If somebody is missing from the roster, check they are **active** on the Employees screen — inactive staff
are excluded.
