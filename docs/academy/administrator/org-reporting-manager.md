# Assigning a reporting manager

## What this feature does

Records who an employee reports to. The name is shown on the employee's profile and in their leave screen.

## Who can use it

Administrators and super administrators.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Employees** and select the person, or use **Organization**.
2. Set the reporting manager.
3. Save.

## Expected outcome

The employee sees their manager's real name on their profile.

## Important: this grants no access

Assigning someone as a reporting manager gives them **no extra permissions whatsoever**. They cannot see
their reports' attendance, and they cannot approve their leave, unless they are an administrator.

Leave approval is an **administrator** action in SkyTrack today.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming a reporting manager can approve their team's leave.** They cannot.
- **Setting the role to "manager" to grant that.** Do not. That role is not functional — it shows the admin
  screens but every action is refused. Use **administrator** if the person genuinely needs to approve leave.
- **Leaving it unassigned.** The employee sees no manager name.
