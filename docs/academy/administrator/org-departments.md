# Managing departments

## What this feature does

Maintains the list of departments employees can belong to. Departments group people for filtering and
reporting.

## Who can use it

Administrators and super administrators.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Open **Organization**.
2. Find the departments section.
3. Add, rename or remove as needed.
4. Assign employees to a department on their own record.

## Expected outcome

Departments appear wherever employees are listed or filtered.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting a department to carry permissions.** It does not. Access is decided by **role**, never by
  department.
- **Renaming a department mid-month and expecting history to change.** The employee's current department is
  what is shown.
